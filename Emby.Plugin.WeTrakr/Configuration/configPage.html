WeTrakr

Link each Emby user with their WeTrakr account. Plays are scrobbled live, and the library is kept in sync both ways: watched history, resume points, ratings, favorites and your Planning list (as a "WeTrakr Watchlist" playlist).

Accounts

Settings

Off: unmarking in Emby (for example to rewatch a show) stays in Emby and does not clear the watch in WeTrakr. WeTrakr never deletes your history either way.

Library sync

The whole library is sent once, on the first sync. After that only what changes in Emby is sent, as it happens.

Checked with one small request when someone starts using Emby and every 30 minutes while in use; a full reconciliation runs once a day (Scheduled Tasks > WeTrakr).

What is imported and sent back in detail (watched, progress, ratings, favorites, Planning) is set per account in WeTrakr: Settings > Emby.

Ignore libraries

Nothing in a checked library is scrobbled or synced, in either direction.
